# Privacy pass (do this before any profile edit)

Why first: by default LinkedIn may announce profile edits (a new role, a work anniversary) to the owner's whole network.
That network can include their current manager. The owner also decides how much a stranger sees. Settle both before
changing anything.

Menu labels drift, so look for each setting by what it does. For each one, tell the owner the current value, recommend a
value with a one-line reason, change it only on their yes, and then reload the page and confirm. Record the results in
`career-notes.md` → *Privacy settings*.

## Settings, in order

1. **Share profile updates with your network** → **Off**.
   Path: Me → Settings & Privacy → Visibility → "Share profile updates with your network" (it may say "Share job
   changes, education changes, and work anniversaries"). This covers job-change notifications.
2. **Per-form "Notify network" toggles** → keep **Off** every time. The Add/Edit position form (and sometimes the
   education, project and certification forms) has its own "Notify network" switch. Check it on every form before
   saving, even after step 1.
3. **Public profile visibility** (what people who aren't signed in and search engines see) → **owner's choice**.
   Path: Me → Settings & Privacy → Visibility → "Edit your public profile" (right-hand panel). Present the options in
   plain words:
   - *Visible* helps recruiters find you through Google. Choose it when you are applying.
   - *Off, or limited sections* is quieter while you are still polishing or if you don't want your employer to notice.
   If they choose quiet now, a sensible limited setting is: profile visible, photo, headline and current position
   shown, and the other sections (About, past roles, education, skills) hidden. Add a dated reminder to
   `career-notes.md`: "Turn public visibility back on when you start applying."
4. **Open to Work**, only if they are job-hunting. Agree the visibility here and switch it on in step 5, together
   with the titles. Choose **Recruiters only** while employed, unless the owner is fine with the employer knowing.
   If the employer must not find out (for example their manager follows them) or the owner is unsure, recommend
   leaving Open to Work **off** for now and searching through applications and the improved profile instead.
   *All LinkedIn members* adds the green #OpenToWork photo frame, which colleagues can see. Recruiters-only is not a
   guarantee: LinkedIn says it tries to hide the signal from recruiters at the owner's own company, but it can't promise
   that. Say so. Title limits and quirks are in [linkedin-gotchas.md](linkedin-gotchas.md).
5. **Optional, only if asked:** profile viewing mode (private browsing hides your name when you view others' profiles)
   and "Profile discovery" by email or phone. Mention these once and don't push.

## Never

- Click any "share", "post" or "share your new position" prompt.
- Change account email, phone, password, two-step verification or other security settings. Those belong to the
  owner, so direct them to do it themselves.
