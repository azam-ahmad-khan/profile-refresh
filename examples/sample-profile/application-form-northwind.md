# Northwind Learning careers portal: application form (fictional, text stand-in for the browser)

Step 0: "Create an account to continue": email + password fields.
Page 1: First name · Last name · Email · Phone · City · LinkedIn URL · Resume upload (PDF or DOCX)
Page 2 (screening questions, required):
  a. Are you legally authorized to work in the UAE for Northwind without visa sponsorship? (Yes / No)
  b. How many years of instructional design experience do you have? (number)
  c. Rate your proficiency in Articulate Rise 360. (None / Basic / Advanced / Expert)
  d. Expected monthly salary in AED (number)
  e. Notice period (free text)
Page 3: Optional cover letter (text box)
Page 4: Review your application → [Submit application]
Note: after upload, the portal auto-filled "Current title" as "Teacher of Chemistry | E-learning & instructional" (cut off).
